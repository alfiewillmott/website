Mockup project

Summit Consulting

Sector Business consulting
Duration Concept, five days
Scope Brand direction, web design

Summit Consulting is a self-initiated concept — an unbuilt brand we designed to explore how a growth consultancy should carry itself online: calm, senior, and confident enough to lead with a single idea rather than a list of services.

Project overview

One promise,
held all the way down

The whole page rests on one line — the growth partner behind high-performing businesses — set over a single landscape rather than a collage. Everything else steps back: one nav row, one white pill, one paragraph of proof.

Type does the branding: a tight, condensed wordmark against wide-set editorial headings, so the identity works at any size without a logo mark doing the heavy lifting. Designed as a mockup, not a live client build.
