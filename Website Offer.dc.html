What the £499 covers

What exactly do I get for £499?

A complete website designed around your business — structure, copy layout, imagery, mobile, contact forms and analytics — designed and built in a fixed seven-day sprint with two review points.

What does the £85 a month cover?

Hosting, domain management, SSL and security patching, backups, uptime monitoring and unlimited content updates — sent to us any time and done within 24 hours.

Why is this cheaper than your usual pricing?

The build fee is low because we keep the relationship long term through hosting. It is the same team and the same process as our £4,500+ projects, capped at three builds a month.

Can I cancel the monthly fee?

Yes, with 30 days’ notice after the first 12 months. You keep your domain and we hand over the site files.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief, your logo files if you have them, and any copy or imagery you want used. If something is missing we will write and source it with you on the kick-off call.
